How it decides

Thirteen airlines, read exactly

Air India, IndiGo, Emirates, Akasa, British Airways and the rest have a reader of their own that knows where the number sits on their page.

Everything else, read by its heading

Any other invoice is searched for headings like Invoice No, Bill No or Credit Note No, and the value beside or below it is taken.

Credit notes count too

A credit or debit note is a document in its own right and is named after its own number, exactly as an invoice is.

It never guesses

A file it cannot read keeps its old name and is listed with the reason. A wrongly named invoice is worse than one left alone, because nobody notices it.
